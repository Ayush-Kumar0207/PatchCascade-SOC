Reward Shaping Formula

Rt = Φ(St-1) − Φ(St) − 0.1 + Rterminal
Φ(S)= Risk Penalty + Downtime Penalty (potential function)
−0.1= Time pressure (ensures dense non-zero reward every step)
Rterminal= +50 (victory) | −100 (catastrophe) | 0 (ongoing)
Based on: Ng, Harada & Russell (1999) — Policy invariance under reward transformations
